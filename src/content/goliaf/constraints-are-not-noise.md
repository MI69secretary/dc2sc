> **Mandatory Boundary Notice:**
> GOLIAF is a bounded travel stress-test environment. It is not presented as a booking service, discount program, travel pool, supplier network, membership, waitlist or proven market mechanism.

## Methodological Stance

Traditional group-buying platforms view participant constraints as friction: they force individuals to conform to standardized packages, fixed departure times, or rigid terms in exchange for volume discounts.

In DC2SC research:
- **Constraints define the boundary of agency:** A participant's non-negotiable constraints (budget ceiling, accessibility, visa limits, cancellation requirements) represent their rights floor.
- **Overriding constraints produces coerced coordination:** Forcing participants to sacrifice constraints for price breaks converts voluntary market agency into platform lock-in.
- **Constraint divergence is a legitimate reason not to aggregate:** When participant constraints cannot be harmonized without rights violation, the protocol must terminate with a "do not aggregate" outcome.
