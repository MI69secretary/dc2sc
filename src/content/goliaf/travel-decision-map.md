> **Mandatory Boundary Notice:**
> GOLIAF is a bounded travel stress-test environment. It is not presented as a booking service, discount program, travel pool, supplier network, membership, waitlist or proven market mechanism.

## Constitutional Distinction

**Participant Journey ≠ DC2SC Constitutional Mechanism.**

The Travel Decision Map models how an individual traveler conceptualizes, plans, and evaluates travel. It is strictly an observational reference to understand where friction occurs, not a specification for an automated transaction funnel.

## The Eight Decision Stages

```text
[01. Intent Horizon] → [02. Constraint Identification] → [03. Window Definition] → [04. Exploration]
        │
        ▼
[05. Option Comparison] → [06. Decision Point] → [07. Execution] → [08. Post-Trip Evaluation]
```

1. **Intent Horizon:** Initial formulation of travel interest, budget parameters, and seasonal window.
2. **Constraint Identification:** Explicit mapping of non-negotiable boundaries (visas, work commitments, medical/family constraints).
3. **Window Definition:** Establishment of acceptable calendar bounds and location tolerances.
4. **Exploration:** Review of market options, availability, and potential coordination with others.
5. **Option Comparison:** Direct evaluation between collective position terms and individual retail alternatives.
6. **Decision Point (Exit Gate):** Unilateral decision to ratify, exit to individual outside option, or abort.
7. **Execution:** Direct independent booking/transaction with service providers.
8. **Post-Trip Evaluation:** Assessment of whether coordination yielded net welfare gain or coordination drag.
