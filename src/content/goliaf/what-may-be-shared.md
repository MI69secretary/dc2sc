> **Mandatory Boundary Notice:**
> GOLIAF is a bounded travel stress-test environment. It is not presented as a booking service, discount program, travel pool, supplier network, membership, waitlist or proven market mechanism.

## The Coordination Boundary Matrix

| Parameter | Collective / Negotiable | Strictly Individual / Protected | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Destination Region** | Negotiable | — | Shared geographic focus enables collective supplier positioning. |
| **Date Windows** | Negotiable range | Exact personal availability | Range defines collective envelope; personal dates remain private. |
| **Budget Envelope** | Target aggregate range | Maximum private willingness-to-pay | Revealing private maximum willingness-to-pay invites supplier price extraction. |
| **Room Quality Standard** | Agreed minimum tier | Personal accessibility / medical needs | Specific medical or accessibility requirements must never be compromised or exposed. |
| **Cancellation Terms** | Ratified minimum floor | Personal emergency thresholds | Collective position requires baseline terms; individuals retain outside options. |
| **Exit Rights** | Unanimous invariant | Unilateral execution | The right to exit cannot be subject to majority vote or group permission. |

## Privacy and Rights Invariants

Information sharing in collective market formation must be strictly asymmetric:
- **Parameters needed for negotiation** (e.g. collective headcount, desired amenities, target arrival window) are exposed to suppliers.
- **Private valuation and personal constraints** (e.g. identity, private reservation alternatives, urgent timelines) remain quarantined on the participant side.
