> **Mandatory Boundary Notice:**
> GOLIAF is a bounded travel stress-test environment. It is not presented as a booking service, discount program, travel pool, supplier network, membership, waitlist or proven market mechanism.

## Why Travel?

In mechanism design, stress-testing a coordination protocol requires choosing a domain where coordination is extraordinarily difficult rather than easy:

1. **Extreme Perishability:** An unsold hotel room or flight seat loses 100% of its value the moment the date passes.
2. **Strict Date Constraints:** A travel itinerary cannot tolerate a 24-hour date mismatch; participants cannot easily substitute dates.
3. **Severe Preference Heterogeneity:** Visa requirements, dietary rules, family obligations, accommodation standards, and budgets differ drastically between individuals.
4. **Strong Outside Options:** Travelers can instantly exit collective coordination to book individually via consumer platforms.

If a buyer-side collective market protocol fails to maintain coherence under these conditions, the failure points reveal where coordination breaks down.

## Research Objectives

- Track the rate at which heterogeneous individual constraints force position dissolution.
- Measure the governance overhead required to negotiate common travel parameters without compromising individual outside options.
