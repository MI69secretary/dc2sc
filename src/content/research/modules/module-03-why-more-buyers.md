## Canonical Purpose

Module 03 dismantles the naive assumption that buyer aggregation automatically results in superior outcomes or structural bargaining power.

## Core Proposition

Volume without ratification, representation, and explicit exit rights reduces buyer flexibility while providing no structural leverage over contract terms. Scale alone is not market power.

## Scope

Analyzes why numerical volume without governance, representation, and exit rights fails to produce durable buyer market power.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not claim aggregation cannot yield short-term promotional discounts.
- Does not assert that scale is universally detrimental.
- Does not model specific supplier pricing algorithms.
