## Canonical Purpose

Module 06 contextualizes GOLIAF as a bounded travel stress-test environment designed to test where collective market formation breaks down under strict constraints.

## Core Proposition

Travel maximizes constraint heterogeneity (dates, visas, medical, family, budget) and outside-option strength, making it an ideal proving ground for testing coordination failure.

## Scope

Defines why high-heterogeneity, high-perishability domains like travel serve as optimal stress-test environments for collective coordination.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not offer booking, travel pooling, group discounts, or supplier intermediation.
- Does not claim travel coordination has been empirically validated.
- Does not operate a live travel service.
