## Canonical Purpose

Module 02 establishes the constitutional boundary between passive volume (crowd dynamics) and active, contestable representation (market positions).

## Core Proposition

Aggregated demand is not equivalent to collective market agency. Attention is reactive, capture-prone, and non-binding; agency requires ratified terms, transparent governance, and enforceable exit rights.

## Scope

Distinguishes between aggregated attention (passive crowd metrics) and ratified collective authority (market agency).

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not claim crowd attention is devoid of economic value.
- Does not demonstrate a specific live market platform architecture.
- Does not assert that crowds cannot achieve temporary promotional price reductions.
