## Canonical Purpose

Module 04 formalizes the distinction between interest, ratification, conditional commitment, and binding commitment, identifying who bears the structural cost of position failure.

## Core Proposition

Ratification is not commitment. Binding participants without clear dissolution rules converts voluntary coordination into coerced lock-in.

## Scope

Examines the taxonomy of commitment types and distributes risk when collective market positions dissolve or fail.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not claim commitment is undesirable.
- Does not specify binding escrow or smart-contract mechanics.
- Does not evaluate live legal enforcement of consumer commitments.
