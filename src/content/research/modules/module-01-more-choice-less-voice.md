## Canonical Purpose

Module 01 defines the foundational problem space of DC2SC: modern digital markets successfully optimize individual transactional choice while systematically dissolving collective bargaining capacity.

## Core Proposition

Individual access expands while structural influence contracts. Independent buyers navigate terms set by platforms or suppliers without mechanisms to negotiate or contest those terms collectively.

## Scope

Defines the structural paradox between expanding individual choice and contracting collective influence over market terms.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not prove collective positions are universally superior to individual choice.
- Does not claim expanding supplier choice is inherently harmful.
- Does not provide live empirical market data.
