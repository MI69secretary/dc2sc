## Canonical Purpose

Module 05 defines the non-negotiable rights foundation that protects independent participants from coercion during collective position formation.

## Core Proposition

Rights floors are non-compensable. Material benefits (e.g. lower prices) cannot legitimize or compensate for the erosion of participant exit, contestability, or privacy rights.

## Scope

Establishes the non-compensable rights floor (exit, contestability, privacy) necessary for legitimate collective market positions.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not provide legal counsel or formal antitrust compliance specifications.
- Does not claim buyer rights unilaterally override supplier contract rights.
- Does not establish statutory enforcement mechanisms.
