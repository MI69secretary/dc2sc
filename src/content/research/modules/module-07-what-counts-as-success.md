## Canonical Purpose

Module 07 defines success metrics that explicitly reject volume or completed transactions as sufficient evidence of participant benefit.

## Core Proposition

Transaction is not success. A valid research methodology must recognize 'do not aggregate', null results, and participant exit as legitimate, complete research outcomes.

## Scope

Defines failure-first evaluation criteria and establishes non-aggregation as a complete, valid research outcome.

## What This Module Does Not Establish

- Does not claim specific benchmarks represent universal welfare improvements.
- Does not evaluate live commercial platforms or transactions.
- Does not claim empirical validation of welfare outcomes.
