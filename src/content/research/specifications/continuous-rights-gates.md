## Governance Sequence

The Continuous Rights Gates model establishes the mandatory sequence through which any proposed collective market position must transition:

```text
Private intent
→ expressed constraints
→ negotiated common ground
→ proposed collective position
→ participant inspection / contestation / revision
→ representation validity gate
→ explicit ratification
→ conditional commitment
→ commitment validity gate
→ collective market position
→ supplier response, if any
→ offers under transparent terms
→ offer / decision validity gate
→ informed accept / reject / revise / exit
→ valid individual outside-option comparison
→ comparative performance evaluation
→ continue / redesign / stop / do not aggregate
```

## Mandatory Constitutional Distinctions

1. **Similarity ≠ Authority:** Shared conditions never confer the right for an agent, platform, or delegate to represent participants without explicit, uncoerced ratification.
2. **Ratification ≠ Commitment:** Voting to approve a collective position proposal is structurally distinct from executing a binding commitment.
3. **Participant Contestability ≠ Supplier Contestability:** The capacity for participants to challenge, modify, or exit terms internally is non-compensable and fundamentally distinct from supplier bidding mechanics.
4. **Material Benefit Cannot Compensate Rights-Floor Failure:** Lower unit prices or promotional concessions cannot legitimize the erosion of participant privacy, unilateral exit rights, or transparent governance.
