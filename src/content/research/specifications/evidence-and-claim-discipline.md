## The E0–E5 Evidence Ladder

Every assertion, module, and specification in DC2SC is bound to an explicit evidence level:

- **E0 — Conceptual Architecture:** Logical models, constitutional axioms, hypotheses, and structural definitions. (All current DC2SC content is E0).
- **E1 — Contextual Prior Art:** Literature review, historical case studies, and documented precedent in economics, law, and mechanism design.
- **E2 — Observational Data:** Uncontrolled observational data from domain environments (e.g. baseline travel constraint tracking).
- **E3 — Controlled Simulation:** Algorithmic simulations, agent-based modeling, and structured lab experiments.
- **E4 — Pre-registered Field Stress Test:** Bounded field environments operating under pre-registered hypotheses and failure metrics.
- **E5 — Replicated Multi-Domain Evidence:** Independent replication of coordination outcomes across diverse market sectors.

## Claim-State & Research-Role Distinctions

The DC2SC schema maintains strict independence between:

1. **`canonicalState` (Authority):** `frozen` (constitutional baseline) vs `working` (active specification) vs `superseded` (historical/replaced).
2. **`claimState` (Validity):** `active` (current thesis) vs `superseded` (updated) vs `rejected` (empirically/logically disproven).
3. **`researchRole` (Epistemic Role):** `concept`, `hypothesis`, `analytical_framework`, `stress_test_protocol`, `empirical_result`.

## Mandatory Evidence Notice

> **Mandatory Public Statement:**
> All current DC2SC and GOLIAF materials are level E0 conceptual frameworks. No E0 concept or E1 contextual source should be read as evidence that DC2SC or GOLIAF produces a real-world effect, financial benefit, or validated market outcome.
