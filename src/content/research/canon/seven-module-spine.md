## Overview

The DC2SC research program is organized into seven sequential canonical modules. Each module isolates a specific structural paradox, governance requirement, or methodological threshold in the transition from isolated individual demand to evaluated collective market positions.

```text

```

## The Seven Modules

1. **[01 — More Choice, Less Voice](/research/module-01-more-choice-less-voice):** Defines the structural paradox between expanding individual choice interfaces and contracting collective influence over market terms.
2. **[02 — A Crowd Is Attention. A Market Position Is Agency.](/research/module-02-crowd-is-attention):** Distinguishes passive aggregated volume from ratified, contestable collective authority.
3. **[03 — Why More Buyers Do Not Automatically Mean a Better Deal](/research/module-03-why-more-buyers):** Analyzes why scale without governance, representation, and exit rights reduces participant flexibility without producing structural leverage.
4. **[04 — The Cost of Commitment](/research/module-04-cost-of-commitment):** Formalizes commitment taxonomy and risk distribution when collective positions dissolve.
5. **[05 — Coordination Rights](/research/module-05-coordination-rights):** Establishes the non-compensable rights floor (exit, contestability, privacy) required for legitimate market coordination.
6. **[06 — Travel Is a Stress Test](/research/module-06-travel-is-a-stress-test):** Establishes why high-heterogeneity, high-perishability domains serve as rigorous stress-test environments for coordination failure.
7. **[07 — What Counts as Success?](/research/module-07-what-counts-as-success):** Defines failure-first evaluation criteria, validating null results and "do not aggregate" decisions as complete research outcomes.
